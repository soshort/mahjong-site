Questions and answers

How do I play mahjong with a friend?

Open Two players → Duel and invite a friend from the leaderboard, or tap “Find an opponent”. You play on one board online, each on your own phone. In Duet you clear one board together.

Can I play offline?

Yes: all levels and the duel with Mazhobe work without a connection. Online duels, leaderboards and the shop need one.

Is the game free?

Yes. There are no purchases; when hints or undos run out, you can get another by watching a short ad if you want to.

How is it different from classic mahjong solitaire?

Tiles don't vanish from the board in pairs — they go to a shelf with four slots, where pairs meet. Fill the shelf with four different tiles and you lose, so you have to think a move ahead.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Yes. Progress is stored anonymously, without registration, and comes back on a new phone through Google Play Games.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — the game is available on Android in Google Play.

Which languages does it support?

22 languages, including English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
