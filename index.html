What's inside

Online duels

One board for two: whoever collects more pairs wins. Invite friends from the leaderboard or let the game find an opponent.

Duel Mazhobe

No opponent around, or no connection? Play Mazhobe himself — Sleepy, Sly or Reckless. The more reckless he is, the more acorns you win.

A 4-tile shelf

Tiles don't vanish on the board — they go to a shelf with four slots. Two alike make a pair, four different and you lose. Think one move ahead.

Spectacular finishers

14 finishers for the last pair: lightning, a black hole, ink, sakura and more — Mazhobe sells them all for acorns.

Ice, locks and mysteries

Frozen tiles, bronze, silver and gold locks, and a mystery tile that blows the whole board apart.

Daily level

One level for everyone each day. The ghost of the day's best player plays right beside you — overtake it for a bonus.
